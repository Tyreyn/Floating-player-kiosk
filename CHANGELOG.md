# Changelog

## 1.2.1

- Action IDs that Kiosk Satellite accepts. 1.2.0 could not be installed.

## 1.2.0

- Close button on the window. It hides the window without stopping the music, until the next track or **Show player window**.
- **Show on the kiosk too** and **Show only above other apps** actions, so a gesture or Home Assistant can show the window on kiosk pages without their own media controls.
- Slightly wider window to fit the close button.

## 1.1.1

- The plugin page shows Ready once the chosen player is followed, instead of keeping an earlier waiting message.
- A missing Display over other apps permission is reported before anything else.

## 1.1.0

- Renamed to Floating Media Player, with the plugin ID `floating-media-player`.
- The **Player** setting lists the players on your Music Assistant server. **Automatic** follows this kiosk's own player.
- English texts throughout.
- Position settings use number fields instead of sliders.

## 1.0.0

- First release: a draggable Music Assistant mini player above other apps, with previous, play/pause and next buttons and a progress bar.
